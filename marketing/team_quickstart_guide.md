# TuringEdge Recovery OS

## Team Quick-Start Guide

This is the simplest way to understand how the product works so the team can start using, selling, and demoing it.

## 1. What The Product Does

TuringEdge Recovery OS helps a collections team do five things in one system:

1. Upload a portfolio sheet
2. Turn it into a live campaign
3. Let agents work accounts and capture commitments
4. Track follow-up discipline after a PTP or callback
5. Help supervisors understand what is working and what to change

In short: the product takes a lender spreadsheet and turns it into an operating workflow.

## 2. The Core Idea

Most collections tools show calls and dispositions.

This product goes one step further:

- It captures borrower commitments like PTP and callback
- It tracks what happens after that commitment
- It shows managers where leakage is happening
- It gives an AI copilot to suggest next actions

The biggest value is not just calling more. It is following through better after the borrower promises something.

## 4. Simple Product Flow

This is the easiest way to explain the product:

1. A lender gives us a spreadsheet of overdue accounts
2. We upload it in Portfolios and Campaigns
3. We map required fields like customer ID, phone, amount due, and DPD
4. We validate the data and launch a campaign
5. Accounts move into the supervisor queue
6. Agents open accounts in Calling Console and talk to borrowers
7. If the borrower gives a PTP or asks for a callback, the agent saves it
8. The system tracks that commitment and follow-up
9. Managers watch the control room and alerts to see where the operation is slipping
10. The Operations Copilot helps explain what is working and what should change
