# Dispute Policy (Demo)

## If the customer disputes the call or claims harassment
1. Acknowledge the concern calmly.
2. Do not argue. Offer to escalate to a supervisor/team.
3. Capture the reason and category for escalation.
4. Create a ticket with a short summary and the last customer statement.

## If the customer says they already paid
1. Ask for transaction reference/UTR and payment date.
2. Confirm you will verify and update them.
