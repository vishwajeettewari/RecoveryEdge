## Slide 2: The real operating problem

- Headline: `Early buckets are won or lost after the promise`
- Core message: Contact volume is not enough. The leak usually happens when PTPs are not tracked consistently or when missed commitments are not followed up quickly.
- Visual suggestion: A simple six-step process arrow showing `Assigned -> Contacted -> PTP captured -> Missed PTP -> Delayed follow-up -> Roll-forward`
- Speaker note: Anchor the conversation in the buyer's language around early-bucket containment and the critical period after a missed PTP.

  - Launch campaigns and queues

  - Monitor follow-up discipline and containment
  - Let supervisors act with an AI control layer
- Visual: layered architecture diagram with product screenshots
- Speaker note: Use this slide to define the full system before diving into individual screens.
