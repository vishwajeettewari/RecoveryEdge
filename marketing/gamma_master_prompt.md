# Gamma Master Prompt

```text
Create a premium enterprise pitch deck in Gamma for a product called TuringEdge Recovery OS.

This deck is for a potential buyer in collections operations at an NBFC or lender. The buyer specifically cares about early-bucket containment, disciplined PTP tracking, timely follow-ups, and what happens immediately after a missed PTP. The tone must be credible, operational, and executive-ready, not generic AI hype.

Design direction:
- Style: premium enterprise SaaS, sharp, modern, polished, boardroom-ready
- Visual language: navy, electric blue, teal, white, soft gradients, glass-like product panels, clean charts, data callouts, subtle motion-ready compositions
- Avoid: purple-heavy AI aesthetics, cartoon bots, cheesy stock photos, vague “future of AI” language, cluttered investor-deck layouts
- Typography: refined and editorial, high contrast, strong hierarchy, concise copy
- Layout: screenshot-led product storytelling with supporting diagrams, proof points, and workflow visuals
- Use TuringEdge branding prominently but tastefully

Audience:
- COO, collections head, operations leader, recovery head, or senior functional operator
- They are skeptical of dashboards that do not improve discipline
- They care about whether the system can operationalize follow-through after a borrower commitment

Core narrative:
TuringEdge Recovery OS is not just a dialer and not just a dashboard. It is a collections operating system that takes a portfolio sheet, operationalizes it into campaigns and queues, helps agents capture commitments, tracks follow-up discipline, surfaces missed-PTP risk, and uses an agentic operations copilot to explain what is working and recommend governed changes by campaign, bucket, customer, or agent.

Product surfaces that must be represented:
1. Login / branded shell
2. Early Bucket Control Room
3. Operations Copilot
4. Portfolios and campaign launch
5. Supervisor Workbench
6. Calling Console with transcript and commitment capture
7. Alerts and Compliance
8. Reports and download center
9. Integrations and reconciliation
10. Users and role-based access

Must-use product claims:
- Portfolio upload from CSV/XLSX with mapping, validation, preview, and launch
- Calling console captures PTPs and callbacks directly from the operating desk
- Commitment capture feeds follow-up scheduling, missed-PTP alerting, and operational metrics
- The control room tracks discipline, containment, recovery, queue health, and bucket-level performance
- The agentic operations copilot explains what is working, why something is leaking, what to change, and what impact to expect
- Actions can be executed directly or routed through approval-aware governance
- Reporting, compliance, and integrations are part of the same operating system

Differentiation to emphasize:
- Standard tools stop at calling activity and static dashboards
- This product closes the loop from ingest to action
- This product focuses on post-commitment discipline, not just call volume
- This product supports supervisors, agents, compliance, and leadership in one system
- The AI is grounded in real metrics, evidence blocks, confidence, and governed actions

Create a 15-slide deck with the following structure:

Slide 1: Cover
- Title: TuringEdge Recovery OS
- Subtitle: Agentic operations copilot for early-bucket collections
- Supporting line: From portfolio upload to disciplined follow-through, containment, and governed action
- Visual: flagship product mockup using the Operations Copilot hero and the Early Bucket Control Room screenshot
- Include logo and a clean executive-grade product montage

Slide 2: The problem collections teams actually face
- Headline: Early buckets are won or lost in the hours after a promise breaks
- Explain that the real operational leak is not just low contact rates; it is weak discipline after PTP capture and delayed re-engagement after a missed PTP
- Use a simple visual journey: assigned account -> contact -> PTP -> missed PTP -> late follow-up -> roll-forward
- Make this feel operational, not academic

Slide 3: What TuringEdge does
- Headline: One operating system for collections execution, discipline, and control
- Show the product as a layered system:
  - portfolio ingestion
  - queue and calling execution
  - follow-up and alerting
  - control room metrics
  - agentic operations copilot
  - reporting and integrations
- Visual: clean architecture diagram with product screenshots embedded

Slide 4: The end-to-end journey
- Headline: From uploaded portfolio sheet to governed next-best action
- Show this sequence:
  1. upload portfolio
  2. map and validate fields
  3. launch campaign
  4. route work to supervisors and agents
  5. capture PTP or callback
  6. schedule follow-ups and alerts
  7. monitor containment and recovery
  8. let the copilot recommend strategy changes
- Visual: modern horizontal workflow with 6-8 steps and relevant screenshots

Slide 5: Portfolio operationalization
- Headline: A spreadsheet becomes a live campaign in minutes
- Show upload, field mapping, validation preview, exclusions, retry policy, and launch controls
- Emphasize that the buyer can upload a sheet of leads or delinquent accounts and immediately operationalize it
- Visual: screenshot of Portfolios & Campaigns with callouts

Slide 6: The control room the buyer wants to see
- Headline: Prove discipline, not just activity
- Use the Early Bucket Control Room screenshot
- Call out metrics like:
  - accounts assigned
  - contact coverage
  - PTP captured
  - follow-up discipline
  - missed PTP alerts
  - containment rate
  - expected recovery
  - realized recovery
- Add a side note: this is the layer that proves whether the operation is containing early buckets before roll-forward

Slide 7: Operations Copilot
- Headline: Ask what is working. Ask why. Ask what to change next.
- Use the Operations Copilot screenshot as the hero visual
- Explain that the copilot can:
  - explain which campaign or bucket is working
  - compare agents after bucket mix
  - diagnose missed-PTP pressure and follow-up gaps
  - recommend strategy changes
  - estimate impact
  - create governed experiments or approval-aware actions
- Make the copilot feel like a control layer for operations leaders

Slide 8: Grounded AI, not generic AI
- Headline: Every recommendation is evidence-backed
- Show evidence blocks, confidence markers, impact estimates, and direct versus approval-gated actions
- Explain that the AI is grounded in campaign, bucket, agent, and customer metrics

  - question

  - recommendation
  - expected impact
  - action

  - live transcript

  - standard tools

  - call activity vs operational discipline
  - dashboards vs recommended actions
  - siloed tools vs one operating system
  - generic AI vs grounded, governed AI

  1. upload a portfolio sheet
  2. launch a campaign
  3. capture a PTP or callback

- Include a short sentence that the product is built to prove follow-through after commitment capture, not just outreach volume

Slide 14: 90-second product walkthrough
- Headline: See the operating flow in action
- Include a large video thumbnail and a prominent CTA button
- Button text: Open product demo
- Use this placeholder link text exactly somewhere on the slide: [INSERT LOOM OR DRIVE LINK HERE]
- Visual should feel like a high-end product teaser, not a webinar slide

Slide 15: Closing and next step
- Headline: Give your collections team a control layer, not another dashboard
- Sub-points:
  - operationalize uploaded portfolios quickly
  - capture and govern commitments consistently
  - measure discipline after the promise
  - let supervisors change strategy with evidence
  - create a pilot around one early-bucket portfolio
```
